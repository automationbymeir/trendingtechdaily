Why decode design systems?

Every great product runs on a design system. From Google's Material Design to Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, from IBM's Carbon to Shopify's Polaris, these libraries quietly shape the look, feel, and behavior of the apps you use every day. We decode each one — the palettes, the typography, the philosophy, the capabilities — and let Claude weigh in on what each system does best.
